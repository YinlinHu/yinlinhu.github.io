<h2 style="margin: 80px 0px 10px;"></h2>

I’m a staff researcher and engineer at [Magic Leap](https://www.magicleap.com/). Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/cvlab/), working with [Pascal Fua](https://people.epfl.ch/pascal.fua) and [Mathieu Salzmann](https://people.epfl.ch/mathieu.salzmann). 

My work focuses on developing 3D computer vision solutions for object and scene understanding to help bridge the digital and physical worlds, and I am exploring effective strategies for embedding traditional geometry knowledge into modern deep learning techniques.
